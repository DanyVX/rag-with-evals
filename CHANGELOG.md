## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added
- Offline-safe document ingestion, fixed-token chunking, persisted BM25 indexing, and cited evidence-only answers.
- FastAPI `/ask` contract and local `ragx serve` command.
- Retrieval metrics (Recall, Hit, MRR, nDCG), citation validity, abstention metrics, bootstrap confidence intervals, and provenance-rich results.
- Hash-addressed resumable experiments, evaluation/report commands, failure analysis, and reproducibility documentation.
